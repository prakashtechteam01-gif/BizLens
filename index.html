Data dictionary

Understand the metrics used by BizLens.
ⓘ

Net revenue = (quantity − returned units) × unit price. Gross profit = net revenue − net cost of goods. It excludes overheads, tax, shipping, discounts and other expenses.

BizLens prototype · Calculations are based only on the records supplied by the user.
